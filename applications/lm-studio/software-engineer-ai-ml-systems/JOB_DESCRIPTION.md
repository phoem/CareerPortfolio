LM Studio is seeking a well-rounded AI/ML Systems Engineer working at the intersection of machine learning, systems engineering, and product development. The role works closely with model authors and upstream open-source communities such as llama.cpp and MLX to make new local-AI capabilities available to LM Studio users.
