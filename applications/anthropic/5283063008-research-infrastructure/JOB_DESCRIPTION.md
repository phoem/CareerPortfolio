## Role overview

The Research Productivity organization builds infrastructure and systems that accelerate Anthropic's research. The team operates with high autonomy, builds from first principles, iterates quickly, and owns reliability and scalability as research products and workloads evolve.

The engineer will independently scope complex projects, resolve ambiguity, establish cross-organizational alignment, make architectural decisions that shape research infrastructure and tooling, and partner directly with researchers to understand and anticipate their workflows.

## Responsibilities

- Design, build, and scale infrastructure for rapidly increasing usage and changing requirements and workloads.
- Independently lead ambiguous, multi-month technical projects from initial scope through production.
- Drive technical alignment among multiple stakeholders and organizations.
- Make architectural decisions that establish the foundation for research infrastructure and tooling.
- Partner directly with researchers to understand workflows and anticipate changing needs.
- Apply pragmatic, first-principles judgment and use fast feedback loops.
- Own reliability and scalability as system load and complexity increase.
- Establish technical standards and best practices and mentor other engineers.

## Required qualifications

- Experience designing, building, and operating large-scale distributed systems or infrastructure in production.
- Demonstrated ability to independently scope and deliver complex, ambiguous, multi-month technical projects.
- Strong software-engineering fundamentals and hands-on coding ability.
- Experience making architectural decisions that other engineers and teams build upon.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills and the ability to establish alignment across teams.
- Ability to operate effectively in ambiguous, fast-changing environments.

## Preferred qualifications

- Experience building infrastructure or platforms for research or machine-learning workflows.
- Experience addressing reliability and architecture challenges during rapid growth.
- Depth in distributed systems, cloud infrastructure, and infrastructure as code.
- Familiarity with the compute, tooling, and workflows used in large-scale machine-learning research.
- Success in startup-like environments with high autonomy and ownership.
- Experience acting as a technical lead or mentor.

## Education and logistics
