# Why Anthropic

I want to work at Anthropic because I want the next stage of my career to contribute to something consequential. After more than 20 years building software and infrastructure, I know that the work I find most satisfying combines difficult engineering, real operational responsibility, and a purpose larger than simply shipping another product. Advanced AI will have an enormous effect on people and society, and I want to help build it at an organization that treats both technical excellence and societal impact as core responsibilities.

The Inference team is especially compelling because its problems closely match the work I have spent my career doing. At ISPRIME, I architected and operated a 24/7 hosting and CDN platform spanning approximately 3,000-4,000 servers across about 10 locations. I built its high-concurrency C/FreeBSD serving runtime and much of the health-checking, monitoring, networking, security, and operational software around it. That experience taught me to think about performance, routing, capacity, failure, observability, and maintainability as one interconnected system—not as isolated components.

Anthropic would let me apply those lessons to infrastructure serving models used by millions of people while learning deeply from engineers working at the frontier of ML systems. I am excited by the challenge of making inference deployments more reliable, flexible, and efficient across changing workloads, accelerator platforms, and real-world events. I would bring substantial distributed-systems and production-operations experience, a willingness to work outside narrow role boundaries, and the humility to learn the inference-specific parts of the stack where my experience is newer.

Most importantly, I want to finish each day knowing that I helped solve meaningful problems with people who care about how their work affects the world. Anthropic offers the combination of mission, technical difficulty, and responsibility that I am looking for.
